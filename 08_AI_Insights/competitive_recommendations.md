# Competitive Intelligence Recommendations

## Main finding

AW Rostamani Automotive is the closest publicly comparable competitor because it combines multi-brand distribution with service, parts, used cars, fleet, rental, mobility subscriptions and EV brands.

## Strategic implications for Al-Futtaim Automotive

1. Protect the integrated ecosystem advantage by connecting sales, Automall, after-sales, charging and fleet data in one customer view.
2. Treat EV readiness as an operating index, not only a model-count metric. Include charger availability, technician readiness, parts lead time and service satisfaction.
3. Benchmark service waiting time and complaint resolution branch by branch against competitor review signals.
4. Build a used-car transparency proposition around inspection evidence, independent inspection access, warranty and resale confidence.
5. Track digital journeys from virtual showroom to test drive, finance, purchase and service booking.

## Data caveat

Public competitor data does not provide audited revenue, unit sales, margin or true market share. Scores are a structured benchmark for portfolio analysis and should be replaced or calibrated with verified company data.

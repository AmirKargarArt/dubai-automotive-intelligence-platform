# Executive Summary — Al-Futtaim Automotive Dubai Intelligence

## Business problem

How can an automotive group in Dubai improve EV adoption, protect after-sales quality, and manage sales/inventory decisions across branches?

## Portfolio findings

1. The public catalog shows a mixed transition model: ICE, Hybrid and BEV products coexist, so the right strategy is staged electrification rather than an all-at-once switch.
2. Public review data points to after-sales waiting time, service capacity and used-car transparency as the highest-value hypotheses to validate.
3. The UAE policy trajectory and charging targets make EV infrastructure and fleet analytics strategic capabilities, not optional dashboard features.

## Recommended actions

| Priority | Action | KPI | 90-day test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Branch/service control tower | on-time %, CSAT, resolution time | Connect service orders from 3 branches |
| 2 | EV + charging pilot | charger utilization, EV conversion, repeat visits | Pilot 5 chargers and 2 EV campaigns |
| 3 | Used-car quality program | inspection-to-sale conversion, return rate, complaints | Add independent inspection disclosure |

## Data note

Public facts are source-traceable. The daily sales, after-sales and inventory files are synthetic and labelled as such; they demonstrate the analytical model and must be replaced by authorized internal data for real decisions.
